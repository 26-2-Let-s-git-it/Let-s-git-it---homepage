All Scholarships, One Place.

All Scholarships, One Place.

Problem

  • Scholarship information is scattered across multiple websites.
  • Students may miss opportunities due to limited access to information.

Solution

  • Collect university and external scholarships in one platform.
  • Make scholarship information easier to find and compare.

Expected Impact

  • Reduce time spent searching for scholarships.
  • Help students discover more scholarship opportunities.
